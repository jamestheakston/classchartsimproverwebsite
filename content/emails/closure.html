Important Update

Saying goodbye to ClassCharts Improver

Hi everyone,
I am writing to you to inform you that ClassCharts Improver will be removed from the Chrome Web Store on July 31, 2026 and ClassCharts Improver development will be discontinued.
Due to low usage, updating the extension doesn't really seem sustainable. However, the project is not going away entirely—the code will remain fully open-source on GitHub, allowing anyone to build local versions, study or fork the extension.

Action Required

Depending on your settings, please ensure you secure your goals, notes, and profile settings before the shutdown date. If you have cloud sync enabled, your data is available for download at classchartsimprover.pages.dev/datadownload. If you store your data locally in your browser, you will need to copy or export your information manually from the extension settings before July 31, 2026.

Thank you so much for using the extension and for all of your support along the way. It truly means a lot.
Have questions? Contact me at jamesttheakston2@gmail.com.
