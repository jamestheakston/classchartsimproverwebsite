Enhance Your Workflow with GitHub

Connecting your GitHub account is the safest and most efficient way to personalize your ClassCharts Improver experience across all your devices.

Secure Authentication

We use GitHub’s official OAuth2 protocol. This means we never see or store your password. You log in directly through GitHub, and they provide a secure token to verify your identity.

Sync Across Devices

By linking your account, your ClassCharts Improver settings, custom themes, and task filters are backed up to the cloud. Log in on any computer and your setup is ready instantly.

How it works

1

Authorize

Click sign-in and GitHub will ask if you want to grant ClassCharts Improver access to your basic profile.

2

Handshake

GitHub sends us a unique identifier. We create a small, private storage slot on our server just for your settings.

3

Enjoy

Your themes and improvements are now linked to you. They'll follow you wherever you use the extension!

Privacy & Data Policy

Public Profile Information

We only request access to your GitHub username and public ID to create your unique ClassCharts Improver profile.

Email Address

Used solely for account recovery and essential notifications. We do not send marketing emails or share your email with third parties.

ClassCharts Improver NEVER requests access to your private repositories, source code, or organization data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this free? ↓

Yes! GitHub Sign-In and the ClassCharts Improver extension are completely free for students and teachers.

Does GitHub get access to my ClassCharts data? ↓

No. The connection is one-way: we only use GitHub to verify who you are. GitHub has no visibility into your homework, grades, or behavior data on ClassCharts.

What if I don't have a GitHub account? ↓

Creating an account takes 60 seconds at github.com. It's the industry standard for developer tools and ensures your data is handled by a world-class security platform.

Full Control

You can disconnect ClassCharts Improver from your GitHub account at any time via your GitHub Settings. Once revoked, our access is immediately and permanently terminated.
