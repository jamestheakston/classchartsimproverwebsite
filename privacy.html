Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Data Collection

ClassCharts Improver processes the following types of information strictly to provide its features:

  • Website Content: The extension reads text and structural elements from the ClassCharts dashboard to reorganize the homework view and apply visual enhancements.
  • Account Information: If you choose to create an account for synchronization features, we collect your email address, chosen preferences, and custom profile photo.

We do not collect sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like home addresses, authentication credentials for your school portal (passwords), health data, or financial information.

Data Usage & Handling

The data processed by the extension is used exclusively to:

  • Inject CSS and JavaScript to modify the visual layout of the ClassCharts student portal.
  • Categorize homework tasks based on their due dates for better organization.
  • Sync settings and profile customization across devices for users with an active account.

Data Storage

By default, all processing occurs locally on your device. For users who opt-in to the synchronization features:

Account data is stored using Supabase. Our database instance is located in Stockholm, Sweden (EU-North-1 region), ensuring that your configuration data remains within European infrastructure.

Security Disclosure

Please be aware that all synchronized data, including your configuration preferences and custom profile photos, are stored in plain text within our database.

Photos are stored as direct image assets and are not encrypted at rest. While we do not store school credentials or passwords, we strongly advise against uploading sensitive personal photos or entering private data into custom fields.

Data Sharing

We maintain a strict Zero-Sharing Policy:

  • User data is never shared with third parties, including advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not sell user email addresses, preference data, or profile images.
  • The extension does not facilitate any communication between users or third-party tracking.

User Rights

You have full control over your information. You can delete your synced account data at any time through the extension settings or by uninstalling the extension. Removing the extension will immediately stop all local data processing.
