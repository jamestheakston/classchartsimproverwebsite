Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

Transparency Disclosure

This policy outlines how ClassCharts Improver collects, uses, handles, and shares user data. We are committed to full transparency and ensuring you understand exactly what happens to your information when using our extension. This policy should be read alongside any in-product disclosures presented within the extension itself.

1. Data We Collect

The extension processes the following categories of information:

  • ClassCharts Website Content (DOM Data): The extension reads the Document Object Model (DOM) of the ClassCharts student portal to identify homework tasks, behaviour points, and dashboard elements. This data is processed entirely locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • Account Identifiers: If you choose to create a CC Improver account, we collect your email address and a system-generated unique user ID for the purposes of authentication and account management.
  • User Preferences & Settings: Custom configuration choices such as theme selections, layout preferences, and notification settings.
  • Custom Profile Photos: If you choose to upload a custom profile photo, that image is stored as a direct asset linked to your account.

We do not collect passwords, ClassCharts login credentials, browsing history outside of ClassCharts, or precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • UI Customisation: Applying visual styles and reorganising homework layouts based on your preferences.
  • Account Management: Authenticating your identity when you log in and managing your account settings.
  • Cross-Device Sync: Allowing users with accounts to maintain a consistent experience across multiple browsers or devices.
  • Feature Functionality: Mapping school-provided icons to our high-quality replacement set, and enabling all other extension features.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the extension's core functionality.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (UK & EU GDPR)

Where the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) or EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases for processing your personal data:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing your email address and account data is necessary to provide the account and cross-device sync features you have requested.
  • Legitimate Interests: Local processing of ClassCharts DOM content is carried out in our legitimate interest of providing the extension's core functionality, and is done entirely on-device without any data leaving your browser.
  • Consent: The upload of a custom profile photo is based on your explicit consent at the point of upload. You may withdraw this at any time by removing your photo or deleting your account.

4. Data Storage

Data storage varies based on your usage:

Local Storage (No Account)

By default, all settings are stored within your browser's local storage. No data leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data immediately.

Cloud Infrastructure — Supabase (Account Holders Only)

For users who create an account, preference data and profile photos are synced to our cloud infrastructure, hosted by Supabase. The database instance is located in Stockholm, Sweden (EU-North-1 region), within the European Economic Area.

5. Security & Handling Disclosure

Plain Text Storage: Please be aware that synchronised preference data and profile photo links are stored in plain text within our Supabase database. They are not encrypted at the application level, though Supabase provides encryption at rest at the infrastructure level.

Unencrypted Media: Custom profile photos are stored as direct image assets and are not encrypted at the application layer. Users are advised not to upload sensitive or private imagery.

6. All Parties With Access to Your Data

The following is a complete list of all parties that may have access to your data. We do not share, sell, or trade your data with any party not listed here.

James Theakston (Developer)

The sole developer and data controller of ClassCharts Improver. Has administrative access to the Supabase project for maintenance and support purposes.

Supabase, Inc. (Infrastructure Sub-Processor)

Our cloud database and authentication provider. Supabase hosts the account data for users who opt in to the sync feature. Data is stored in their EU-North-1 (Stockholm, Sweden) region. Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and is subject to their own privacy and security commitments. See: supabase.com/privacy.

Google LLC (Browser Platform Provider)

ClassCharts Improver is distributed via the Chrome Web Store, operated by Google LLC. Google may collect data related to your use of the Chrome Web Store (such as install/uninstall events) in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not share any user data with Google directly. See: Google Privacy Policy.

Law Enforcement / Regulatory Authorities

We will only disclose personal data if required to do so under applicable law (UK or Swedish/EU jurisdiction) in response to a valid, verified legal request such as a court order or statutory obligation. We will notify affected users where legally permitted to do so.

No Other Parties

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics networks, data brokers, or any other third parties not listed above. Zero data is shared with advertising or tracking networks of any kind.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide the service or as required by law:

  • Local Storage Data: Retained indefinitely within your browser until you uninstall the extension or manually clear browser data, at which point it is deleted immediately.
  • Account & Cloud Sync Data: Retained for the lifetime of your account. Upon account deletion, all associated data (email, preferences, profile photo) is permanently deleted from our Supabase database within 30 days.
  • ClassCharts DOM Data: Never stored or retained. This data is processed transiently in memory only, solely to render the improved UI, and is discarded immediately after use.
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts with no login activity for more than 24 months may be automatically deleted along with all associated data. We will attempt to notify you via email before doing so.

8. Your Rights & Data Control

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your account and all associated cloud data at any time by contacting the developer.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent (e.g. profile photo), you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the developer using the details below. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. For material changes, we will endeavour to notify account holders via email. Continued use of the extension after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
