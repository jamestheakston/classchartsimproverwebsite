Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 18, 2026

At ClassCharts Improver, we believe that your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our browser extension and associated services.

1. Data Storage and Location

We utilize two tiers of data storage depending on your account status:

Local Storage: By default, all preferences and data are stored locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your browser unless you choose to sync it.

Cloud Storage: If you create an account, your data (profile photo, accent colour, goals, and notes) is moved to our cloud server located in Stockholm, Sweden to enable cross-device syncing.

Security Warning

Please be aware that your profile photo, accent colour, goals, and notes are currently stored in plain text on our servers. While we take measures to protect our infrastructure, these specific data points are not encrypted at rest and may not be fully secure. We advise against storing sensitive personal information or passwords within the notes or goals sections.

2. Data Collection and Usage

No School Credential Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit your ClassCharts passwords or official school credentials.

No Tracking: We do not use cookies or tracking pixels to monitor your browsing habits outside of the extension's specific functionality.

3. Interaction with ClassCharts

The extension works by injecting CSS and JavaScript into the official ClassCharts website to improve your user experience locally.

Any data displayed (such as homework tasks or points) is fetched directly from ClassCharts by your browser and is never intercepted or stored on our Stockholm server.

4. Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • storage: To save your UI preferences and sync them with our Stockholm server.
  • identity: To securely manage your user account and authenticate cloud-syncing features.
  • host permissions: To allow visual improvements specifically on ClassCharts domains.
