Remove ClassCharts Improver

Follow these simple steps to remove ClassCharts Improver from your Google Chrome browser:

  1. 1 Open Google Chrome on your computer.
  2. 2 Next to the URL bar, click the puzzle icon.
  3. 3 Click the Kebab menu (three dots) next to the ClassCharts Improver option.
  4. 4 Click the Remove button.
  5. 5 Confirm by clicking Remove again in the pop-up.

Tip: If you see an extension icon in your browser toolbar, you can also right-click it and select "Remove from Chrome" for a faster process.
