Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my beta access code?

Your beta access code is displayed in the Overview tab after you complete enrollment. You can also find it in your account settings.

Can I use the beta on multiple devices?

Yes, your beta access code works across all devices where you have the ClassCharts Improver extension installed.

What happens if I leave the beta?

Leaving the beta will remove your access code and disable beta features. You can re-enroll at any time to regain access.

How do I report bugs or suggest features?

Use the Feedback tab to submit bug reports, feature requests, or general feedback. We appreciate your input!

Is my data safe during the beta?

Absolutely. We use the same security measures as the main ClassCharts Improver platform. Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. Your data is encrypted with enterprise grade protection.

Beta Privacy Policy

Note: This privacy policy applies only to the ClassCharts Improver Beta program, not the full extension.

Data Collection: We collect your email address and name for beta enrollment purposes only.

Data Storage: Your data is stored securely in our database (Supabase) and is encrypted.

Data Usage: Your information is used solely to manage your beta access and communicate about beta updates.

Feedback Submission: Any feedback you submit through the Feedback tab is sent via Formspree. We encourage you to review both Formspree's Privacy Policy and Supabase's Privacy Policy to understand how your data is handled.

Data Sharing: We do not share your personal data with third parties.

Data Retention: Your data will be retained while you are enrolled in the beta program. You may request deletion at any time by leaving the beta.
